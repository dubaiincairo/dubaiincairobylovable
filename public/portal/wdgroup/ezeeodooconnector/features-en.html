SYNC-01 1. GL Architecture

Dual Operating Mode Architecture

What is this feature:

Architectural selection on pms.credentials between Journal Daily Summary (aggregates raw API feeds into one balanced GL entry per hotel/day) and Detailed Transactions (posts individual customer invoices and payments). The connector strictly prohibits mixing modes in the same period.

Real Life Benefit:

Eliminates tens of thousands of redundant folio invoices that previously clogged Odoo accounting databases, reducing database bloat by over 90% while keeping financial ledgers perfectly balanced.

SYNC-02 1. GL Architecture

General Manager Report Layout Parity

What is this feature:

Renders a native, bilingual HTML tab (ezee_manager_layout_html) directly inside the Odoo journal entry mirroring the official eZee General Manager report sections: Room Charges, Extra Charges, Municipality Fee 2.5%, VAT 15%, Payments, City Ledger, and Guest Ledger.

Real Life Benefit:

Hotel Controllers and General Managers can verify daily postings in seconds without deciphering multi-line accounting debits and credits, matching the daily PMS printout 1:1.

SYNC-03 1. GL Architecture

Balanced Multi-Leg Journal Posting Engine

What is this feature:

Automated multi-leg journal compilation algorithm that groups raw transactional records into distinct debit legs (MADA, Visa, Cash, Bank Transfer, City Ledger Transfers) and credit legs (Room Revenues, Laundry, Cafe, VAT, Municipality Fees).

Real Life Benefit:

Guarantees zero out-of-balance entries in Odoo, ensuring that total debits always strictly equal total credits down to the final halala before posting is committed.

SYNC-04 1. GL Architecture

Government Fees & VAT Tax Segregation

What is this feature:

Built-in classification parser (manager_classification.py) that distinguishes between Saudi Municipality Hospitality Fees (2.5%), standard Value Added Tax (VAT 15%), and base revenues, routing each to its specific GL liability or expense account.

Real Life Benefit:

Ensures 100% compliance with Saudi ZATCA and Balady regulations, preventing municipal fees from inflating taxable VAT bases or distorting net operational revenue.

SYNC-05 1. GL Architecture

eZee Daily Rounding Adjustment Line

What is this feature:

Automated residual balancer that calculates microscopic fractional differences (±0.01 to ±0.05 SAR) resulting from multiple decimal roundings in tax formulas, posting the offset to eZee Daily Rounding.

Real Life Benefit:

Prevents daily synchronization crashes caused by strict Odoo balanced-move constraints when eZee calculations round tax lines independently across hundreds of guest transactions.

SYNC-06 1. GL Architecture

Base Currency & Multi-Currency Detection

What is this feature:

Automatically reads the hotel currency configuration (e.g. SAR) from the eZee API during authentication handshake and validates parity with the target Odoo company currency.

Real Life Benefit:

Guards against catastrophic currency mismatches across hotel properties, ensuring foreign exchange rate conversions are handled accurately without corrupting company financial statements.
