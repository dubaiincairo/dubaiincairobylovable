Ideal Customer Profiles (ICP) & Persona Matrix Pillar 01

Precision targeting maps prospective customers into 4 distinct Saudi high-intent brackets, preventing wasted ad impressions and driving superior conversion value.

Swiss Blue Hotels Corporate & Extended Stay

The Riyadh Executive & Consultant

Demographics: Age 30–52, Saudi & GCC professionals, management consultants, engineers on 2–6 week project assignments in Riyadh.

Primary Motivators: Ultra-fast Wi-Fi, quiet workspace, kitchenettes for meal flexibility, seamless corporate invoicing, location proximity to Olaya/KAFD.

Target Acquisition Channel: Google Search ("شقق فندقية شمال الرياض"), LinkedIn Sponsored Updates, Google Hotel Ads.

Swiss Blue Hotels Leisure & Family

The GCC Weekend Family & Staycationer

Demographics: Families from Eastern Province, Jeddah, Kuwait, UAE visiting Riyadh for Riyadh Season, medical appointments, or family gatherings.

Primary Motivators: Multi-room suites, privacy, cleanliness, free covered parking, children-friendly living spaces.

Target Acquisition Channel: Snapchat AR Filters, Instagram Reels showcase, TikTok family travel creator partnerships.

WD Group Luxury Retail / HNWI

The Private Villa Owner (Saudi HNWI)

Demographics: Age 35–60, Saudi nationals building or refurbishing high-end private villas in Riyadh (Al-Hada, Al-Malqa, Al-Nakheel).

Primary Motivators: Solid wood craftsmanship, bespoke sizing, weather-resistant exterior doors, 25-year structural warranty, prestige.

Target Acquisition Channel: Meta Advantage+ Catalog, Instagram high-aesthetic Reels, wood sample swatch box lead magnet.

WD Group Commercial B2B

The Architectural Specifier & Contractor

Demographics: Interior design directors, hospitality fit-out managers, procurement consultants executing boutique hotels and corporate offices.

Primary Motivators: Strict BOM adherence, factory production capacity (3 plants), fire-rated doors, ZATCA Phase 2 compliance, credit terms.

Target Acquisition Channel: LinkedIn ABM campaigns, Google B2B Search ("توريد ابواب خشب فنادق"), industry whitepapers.
