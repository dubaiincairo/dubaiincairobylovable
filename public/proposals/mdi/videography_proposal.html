  • Execution of approved scripts strictly within the agreed shooting schedule.
Phase 3: Post-Production & Mastering Post-Production
  • Offline & online editing of 3 videos (~60 seconds each).
  • Cinematic colour correction and precise colour grading.
  • Integration of client-supplied final OneBank animations.
  • Sound engineering, dialogue cleanup, sound design & final audio mix.
  • Licensed background music cleared for agreed channels.
  • Application of approved branding, titles, captions & end cards.
