Operational Governance, SLA & Commercial Terms

1. Client Responsibilities

  • Supply brand guidelines, logos, approved messaging, and reference files.
  • Provide final approved animation assets and client-owned production materials.
  • Appoint a designated representative to consolidate feedback and sign off each stage.

2. Production, Revisions & Exclusions

  • Photography Revisions: 2 consolidated rounds for image selection and retouching.
  • Video Revisions: 2 consolidated editing rounds per video.
  • Consolidated feedback provided through OneBank designated lead.
  • Digital delivery of high-resolution JPEGs and MP4 video masters.

The photography and video services are complementary components of one integrated, non-separable quotation. Itemised amounts are provided solely for cost transparency and payment calculation. Acceptance applies to the complete package. Neither component may be booked independently.

  • Strict confidentiality applied to OneBank-supplied unreleased assets prior to official launch.
  • Quotation Validity: 15 calendar days from quotation date (5 October 2026).
