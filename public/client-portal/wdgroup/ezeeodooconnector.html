Ozoo eZee-Odoo Connector

Automated daily revenue, folio, and payment reconciliation pipeline bridging eZee Absolute PMS with Odoo 19 Enterprise.

01

Automated Daily Sync

Daily cron job triggering at 03:00 AM AST pulls reconciled night audit folios from eZee Absolute and maps room revenues, taxes, and service fees.

02

Journal Entry Creation

Automatically balances debit (cash, POS, payment gateway, receivables) and credit (room revenue, F&B, VAT 15%) in Odoo accounting journals.

03

Reconciliation Ledger

Generates comprehensive audit logs with discrepancy detection, preventing duplicate postings and alerting accounting managers of variance.
