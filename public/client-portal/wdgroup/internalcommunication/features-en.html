Pillar 1: Multi-Domain Identity & Cryptographic DNS Hardening

5 Capabilities
FEAT-COM-01 Email Cryptography

2048-Bit DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail) Cryptographic Key Signing

What is this capability?

Asymmetric cryptographic signing using 2048-bit RSA keys published in Cloudflare DNS TXT records. Every outbound email from any holding domain is stamped with a unique cryptographic signature in the DKIM-Signature header.

Real Life Benefit

Guarantees message integrity during transmission. Eliminates recipient spam categorization by Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and government mail servers, ensuring multi-million riyal commercial quotes land directly in client inboxes.

FEAT-COM-02 Sender Authorization

Strict Multi-Domain SPF (Sender Policy Framework) Alignment

What is this capability?

DNS TXT SPF records (v=spf1 include:zoho.com ~all) explicitly whitelisting authorized outbound IP subnets across all group domains (wdgroup.online, swissbluehotels.com, etc.).

Real Life Benefit

Prevents malicious actors from forging employee email addresses to send unauthorized purchase orders or fake payment requests to corporate suppliers and banking partners.

FEAT-COM-03 Anti-Spoofing Policy

DMARC Policy Enforcement & Forensic Deliverability Reporting

What is this capability?

Active DMARC record (v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:...) enforcing alignment between SPF and DKIM identifiers, with automated XML forensic telemetry sent to IT security.

Real Life Benefit

Provides executive brand immunity against CEO fraud and spoofing attacks. Automatically instructs receiving global mail servers to isolate unauthorized messages attempting to impersonate holding directors.

FEAT-COM-04 Mail Routing Redundancy

High-Availability MX Records & Zero-Loss Dual-Redundant Routing

What is this capability?

Dual-tiered MX records (mx.zoho.com priority 10, mx2.zoho.com priority 20, mx3.zoho.com priority 50) backed by Cloudflare DNS Anycast routing.

Real Life Benefit

Eliminates bounced emails and data loss during localized internet outages or maintenance windows, maintaining 99.9% business continuity for hotel bookings and factory logistics.

FEAT-COM-05 Holding Infrastructure

Multi-Domain Holding Consolidation Under Single Administrative Tenant

What is this capability?

Unified Zoho Workplace organization managing multiple brand domains under a single administrative umbrella, with cross-domain user routing and shared infrastructure.

Real Life Benefit

Reduces software licensing overhead by over 50% compared to maintaining isolated email tenants, while enabling holding executives to communicate seamlessly across all subsidiaries.
